## How do the shuttles work?

Free shuttles run between the hotels and every event on Friday and Saturday, so nobody needs to drive. Find where you're staying below and be at the pick-up spot about 5 minutes early.

**Staying on or near Warren Street?** This includes The Maker Hotel, Airbnbs and other places in town. Shuttles stop at the corners of Warren St and 7th, 5th, 3rd and 1st Streets, in that order. The Maker is at the 3rd St stop.

### Friday, December 4: Welcome dinner at The Caboose, 60 S Front St

**Getting to dinner**
- **voco James Newbury Hotel, Coxsackie:** board at 5:30 PM (departs 5:45, arrives about 6:15)
- **Pocketbook Hudson:** 6:00 PM
- **Warren St stops & The Maker:** 6:10 PM, with a second pick-up at 6:35 PM
- **Hudson Navigator:** 6:20 PM

**Getting home**
- **Hudson hotels & Warren St:** shuttles run back and forth from 9:00 to 10:30 PM
- **voco, Coxsackie:** 9:00 PM (may leave as late as 9:30). This is the only ride back to Coxsackie.

*Wedding party:* your shuttle picks up at Pocketbook Hudson at 5:10, The Maker at 5:20 and Hudson Navigator at 5:30 PM. It takes you to St. Mary's Church first, then on to dinner.

### Saturday, December 5: Ceremony at 12:30 PM at St. Mary's Church, 429 E Allen St. Reception at Basilica Hudson, 110 S Front St.

**Getting to the ceremony**
- **voco James Newbury Hotel, Coxsackie:** board at 11:20 AM (departs 11:30, arrives about 12:00)
- **Pocketbook Hudson:** 11:40 AM or 12:10 PM
- **Hudson Navigator:** 11:55 AM
- **Warren St stops & The Maker:** shuttles loop from 11:40 AM until the ceremony. Board by 12:15 PM.

**After the ceremony**
- **Straight to the reception:** a shuttle leaves St. Mary's at 2:00 PM and drops off at the north side of Basilica Hudson about 2:15. Relax there until cocktail hour. Coxsackie guests, this is your shuttle.
- **Back to Hudson hotels to rest:** shuttles leave St. Mary's for the Pocketbook at 2:00 PM, for other Hudson hotels at 2:20 PM, and loop to the Warren St stops until 3:00 PM.

**Getting to the reception**
- **Pocketbook Hudson:** 3:00 PM or 3:35 PM (arrives about 3:25 or 3:45)
- **Warren St stops & The Maker:** shuttles loop from 3:00 to 4:00 PM; the first arrives about 3:20
- **Hudson Navigator:** walk to the Warren & 3rd St stop and catch the Warren St shuttle, 3:00 to 4:00 PM

**Getting home**
- **Hudson hotels & Warren St:** shuttles run back and forth from Basilica Hudson, 9:00 to 10:30 PM
- **voco, Coxsackie:** 9:00 PM or 10:00 PM. The 10:00 PM shuttle is the last ride to Coxsackie.
