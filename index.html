How do the shuttles work?

Free shuttles run between the hotels and every event on Friday and Saturday, so nobody needs to drive. Find where you're staying below and be at the pick-up spot about 5 minutes early.

Staying on or near Warren Street? This includes The Maker Hotel, Airbnbs and other places in town. Shuttles stop at the corners of Warren St and 7th, 5th, 3rd and 1st Streets, in that order. The Maker is at the 3rd St stop.
