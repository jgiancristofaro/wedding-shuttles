# Caroline & Joe · Wedding Shuttles

Interactive shuttle guide for the wedding weekend in Hudson, NY (December 4–5, 2026).
Open `index.html` or the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

- Pick a day (Friday welcome dinner / Saturday wedding).
- Tap a hotel or venue to see every pick-up there and every ride back.
- The timeline and stop-by-stop cards show each vehicle's full route and seat count.
- "Watch the day play out" animates every shuttle on a map; press play or drag the slider through Friday or Saturday.

Source: Coxsackie Transport reservations and the shuttle schedule dated October 20, 2026.
